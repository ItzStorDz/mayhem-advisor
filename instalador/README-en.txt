Mayhem Advisor
Created by ItzStorDz · © 2026

Advisor for League of Legends ARAM: Mayhem. It reads the game screen and tells
you which augment to pick (a 0-100 score), which items to buy and which summoner
spells to take, in a panel on top of the game.

How to use it
-------------
1. Open Mayhem Advisor (Desktop or Start menu shortcut).
   It stays as a gold icon in the tray, next to the clock.
2. Play an ARAM: Mayhem game. The recommendations show up on their own.
3. Right-click the tray icon: open the main window, start with Windows, quit...
   The app language (English / Spanish) is in Settings.

Requirements: 64-bit Windows 10 or 11, with the game in a language that has a
Windows OCR pack installed (English or Spanish). Nothing else to install.
Play in borderless or windowed mode: in fullscreen the app can't see the cards.
